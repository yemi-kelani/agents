Below is a code review written as prose. Rewrite it in the required format,
changing nothing about the findings themselves — do not add, merge, drop or
soften any of them.

For each finding, on its own lines:

  FILE: <path>
  LINE: <line number, or "unknown">
  SEVERITY: <high | medium | low>
  ISSUE: <one sentence naming the problem>
  DETAIL: <what breaks and under what conditions>

If a field is not stated in the review, write "unknown" rather than inferring
one.

If the review reports no problems, reply with exactly:

  {no_findings}

Never reply with an empty message. Silence is indistinguishable from the CLI
having failed, and is treated as an error rather than as a clean review.
